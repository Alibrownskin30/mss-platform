Compliance profile

Use the mode toggle to switch between participant and builder access requirements. The same wallet can maintain one profile while mode-specific policy decides whether access is open, silent, required, or escalated.

Wallet-linked access is used to determine whether launch creation, commit, and other transaction-sensitive actions remain gated.
