What is required

The Launcher flow is based on transparent terms and wallet-linked acknowledgement rather than identity verification.

1
Connect or enter the wallet performing the Launcher action.
2
Select participant or builder mode for the correct terms.
3
Read and accept the relevant Launcher terms and risk acknowledgements.
4
Return to the launch or launch creator to continue the transaction.

Operational protection

Wallets can still be prevented from using transaction-sensitive Launcher actions where an internal intervention block has already been applied.

Builder Flow
Acknowledgement Only
Builders accept project accuracy and prohibited conduct terms before launch creation.
Participant Flow
Acknowledgement Only
Participants accept launch risk and transaction conditions before committing.
No ID verification required. A blocked wallet cannot complete Launcher transactions. Where a builder has already paid a Builder Bond before an internal block prevents launch creation, the Builder Bond refund result is surfaced through the launch creation transaction flow.
